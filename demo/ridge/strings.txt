285	About drain cleaning
286	Will chemical drain cleaner fix it?
287	Sometimes for a while, but it eats old pipes and rarely shifts a real block. We'd rather you didn't: it also burns whoever opens the pipe next.
288	How long does it take?
289	Most sink and shower drains take under an hour. A main line with roots can take two or three.
